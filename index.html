About

A working kitchen, not a concept.

Al Fairoz Kebabish has been cooking on Sheep Street for years. The room is small, the menu is honest, and the kitchen is busy. Most of what we cook goes home in a foil tray; a few people sit in for a curry and a cup of chai.

The grill is real charcoal — lamb chops, chicken tikka and seekh kebabs come off the coals with the colour and the smoke that only that heat gives. The karahis are cooked one pan at a time, the way they are at home. The naans are pulled out of a clay tandoor a few feet from where you wait.

We are a few doors from the bus station, with the bus crowd on one side and the Saturday-night Northampton lot on the other. Different orders, same kitchen, same coals.

The Menu

From the grill, from the karahi, from the tandoor.

From the charcoal grill

Marinated overnight, fired hard over real coals.

  • Chicken tikka boneless · on skewers
  • Tikka boti diced lamb, marinated

  • Seekh kebab minced lamb, hand-pressed

From the karahi

Cooked to order in the small steel pan that gives the dish its name.

  • Chicken karahi tomato, ginger, green chilli
  • Lamb karahi slow-cooked on the bone
  • Butter chicken creamy, lightly spiced
  • Methi gosht lamb with fenugreek leaf
  • Daal tarka yellow lentils, tempered
  • Saag paneer spinach & soft cheese

From the tandoor

  • Roti wholemeal, fresh
